The **knight** could **write**.

He **wrote** on a **scroll** with a quill.

He **knew** all the words in the land.

One day he **knocked** on the king's door.

"I have **written** you a letter," said the **knight**.

The king read the letter and smiled.

"**Kneel** and I will make you my scribe."
