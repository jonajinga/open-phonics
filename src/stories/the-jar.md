**Jim** had a **jar** of **jam**.

But the **jar** was up **far** too high!

**Jim** got on a **big** box.

He got on his tip toes.

He got the **jar**!

But then — he fell off the box!

The **jar** of **jam** fell too.

**Jam** got on **Jim**. **Jam** got on the rug.

**Jam** got on the **cat**!

"**Yum**!" said **Jim**. He **ate** the **jam** off his hand.

The **cat** **ate** the **jam** off the rug.
