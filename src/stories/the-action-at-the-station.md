There was **action** at the **station**.

A man had to make a **decision**.

"Which **train** do I take?" he said.

He had a **vision** of the sea.

"I will go to the coast," he said.

He got on the **train** with a **motion** of his hand.

The **train** left the **station**.

It was the best **decision** he ever made.
