**Joy** went to the **toy** **shop**.

She saw **coins** and **trains** and dolls.

"Which **toy** do you **enjoy** the most?" said Mom.

**Joy** **pointed** at a **small** drum.

"That one!" she said with a **smile**.

She **joined** the line to pay.

**Joy** played her drum all the way home.
