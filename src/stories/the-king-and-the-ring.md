The **king** had a **big** **ring**.

One **day** the **ring** fell off **his** hand.

"**My** **ring**! **My** **ring** is gone!" the **king** said.

The **king** told **his** **men** to **look** for it.

The **men** did **look** and **look**.

One man found the **ring** in the **mud**.

"I got it!" the man said.

The **king** put the **ring** on **his** hand.

He was so glad he did **sing** a **song**.

All the **men** did **sing** with the **king**.
