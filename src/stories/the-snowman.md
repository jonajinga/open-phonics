The **snow** fell **slow** and soft.

I **knew** it was time to go out.

I **threw** on my coat and **gloves**.

I made a **snowman** in the yard.

He had a **brown** hat and a long **scarf**.

A few **crows** sat on his arms.

"I **know** you will melt," I said, "but for **now**, you are my friend."
