I heard a **loud** **sound**.

I **looked** **out** the **door**.

A **big** **hound** was **running** **round** and **round** the yard!

He ran so fast his ears went up and **out**.

Then he stopped. He put his nose on the **ground**.

He **found** something! A **mouse**!

The **mouse** **ran**. The **hound** **ran**.

The **mouse** hid. The **hound** could **not** find it.

The **hound** sat **down** and let **out** a **loud** howl.

I think the **mouse** won.
