A **lamb** **sat** on a **hill**.

A **gnat** **sat** on the **lamb**.

The **lamb** did **not** like the **gnat**.

The **lamb** did **climb** the **hill** to get rid of the **gnat**.

A **ghost** came from the fog.

The **gnat** got scared and left.

The **lamb** **sat** on the **hill** in the sun.
