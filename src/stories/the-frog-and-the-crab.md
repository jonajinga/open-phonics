A **crab** **crept** from the **creek**.

"What are you doing?" said the **frog**.

"I am looking for a **snack**," said the **crab**.

The **frog** did a **big** **flip** into the **stream**.

**Splash**! The **crab** got **wet**.

"Do it again!" said the **crab**.
