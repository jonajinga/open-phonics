A **kitten** and a **rabbit** lived on a hilltop.

The **kitten** was quick and playful.

The **rabbit** was calm and **clever**.

One sunset, they went to the pond.

An **insect** buzzed on the water.

The **kitten** tried to catch it but fell in!

The **rabbit** pulled the **kitten** out.

"Thank you," said the **kitten**. "You are a good friend."
