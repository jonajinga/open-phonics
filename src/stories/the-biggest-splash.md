Kim ran to the **pond**.

She made a **big** **splash**.

Tom made a **bigger** **splash**.

"I will make the **biggest** **splash**!" said Jim.

Jim ran **quickly** and did a **big** jump.

**Splash**! It was the **biggest** one yet.

Kim walked **slowly** back to try again.

She went **carefully** this time.

She made the **fastest** run and the **biggest** **splash** of all!
