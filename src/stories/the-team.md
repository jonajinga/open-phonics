**Dean** and **Jean** are a **team**.

**Dean** can **read** and **read**.

**Jean** can **leap** **far**.

One day the **rain** came.

"**Wait**!" said **Dean**. "I can **read** to you."

So **Dean** **read** a tale to **Jean**.

**Jean** said, "That was the **best** tale!"

A good **team** can do a lot.
