A **man** had a **dog**.

The **dog** was **not** a good **dog**.

The **dog** **ran** in the **mud**.

The **dog** **dug** and **dug**.

The **dog** got **mud** on the **rug**!

"**Not** the **rug**!" the **man** said.

The **dog** **sat** on the **man**.

The **man** got **mud** on him too!

"You are **fun**," the **man** said. "But you are a **mud** **dog**!"
