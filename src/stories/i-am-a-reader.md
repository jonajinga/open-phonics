I am a **reader**.

I **learned** every **sound**.

I can **read** about a cat on a mat.

I can **read** about a boat on a road.

I can **read** about a **knight** who could **write**.

I can **read** about a **little** **turtle** in a **puddle**.

I can **read** about **action** at a **station**.

**Reading** is the best thing in the **world**.

I will **read** every day for the rest of my life.

I am a **reader**!
